Privacy Policy

Hermes Gmail Triage is a personal tool used only by its owner, Jeremiah Kim, on his own Google account.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission it reads email metadata and short snippets (sender, subject, preview) from the owner's Gmail inbox using the read-only Gmail scope. It cannot send, delete, or change email.

How data is used

The data is processed on the owner's own computer to produce a short daily summary. It is not sold, shared, or sent to any third party, and it is not used for advertising.

Storage

Summaries are kept in the owner's private notes on the same computer. No email content is stored on any server operated by this tool.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
